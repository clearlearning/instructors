🍽️ Meal Per Diem
🎓 Instructor per diem: Your full per diem is automatically applied for each day. No receipts are required for meals. If a meal is provided for you by CLEAR or the program host, select the checkbox in the Meal Provided column and indicate which meal or meals was/were provided in the Which Meal(s) drop down menu for that day. This will adjust your per diem amount for that day to reflect any meals not already provided.

💳 Expense Lines
If paying expenses in a currency other than USD, select the correct currency in the provided drop-down menu and enter your expense item in the ORIGINAL CURRENCY field. This will calculate the USD amount at the current exchange rate (shown below the row). Repeat these steps for any line items paid for in a currency other than USD; otherwise, enter your expense amount in the Amount (USD) field.

For categories like Hotel, Airfare, and Rental Car, use the Description field to record the vendor name.
