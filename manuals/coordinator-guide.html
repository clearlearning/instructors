How it fits together

Each quarter we schedule the next quarter's programs, find instructors for them, and give those instructors everything they need for the day they teach. The Instructor Portal now holds that whole process in one place, replacing TeamGantt, the instructor availability spreadsheet and several separate tools.

Timeline for a cohort

Previous quarter: the cohort is scheduled, its agenda generated, and it's published and announced.
Until 30 days before the first live session: instructors respond. The response date is set automatically.
7 and 2 days before the response date: reminders go to approved instructors who haven't responded.
After the response date: answers lock; staff confirm instructors and send selection notices.
By 1 week before the first live session: Zoom links are in place. Anything missing appears on the Overview.
Before the cohort starts: download the cohort kit for the survey links, QR codes and welcome email.
From each instructor's last session: they can submit their expense and hours report.
After the cohort: publish evaluation reports to instructors.

Signing in

The Instructor Portal is protected by Cloudflare Zero Trust, so nobody can open it without signing in.

  1. Open the portal's staff dashboard (/admin-dashboard) or the Program Scheduler (/program-scheduler).
  2. Enter your CLEAR email address and choose Send me a code.
  3. Enter the one-time code from your email. You stay signed in for a while on that browser.
Staff and instructor access are separate. Staff pages only open for addresses in the staff access group. If a new coordinator can't sign in, see Instructor and staff records.

The staff dashboard has seven tabs: Overview, Responses, Selection, Programs, Instructors, Staff and Activity. The header links to the Program Scheduler and to the instructor view.

1. Plan the quarter

All staff work in the same shared schedule. Changes save automatically; the header shows All changes saved when they're stored.

Add the cohorts

  1. Open the Program Scheduler and use the arrows to move to the quarter you're planning.
  2. Choose Add cohort, then the program.
  3. Enter the Welcome session date (or Day 1 for in-person programs). The preview lists every session the template creates, with any holidays flagged.
  4. Choose the start time. Online programs default to 1:00 PM Eastern; 11:00 AM and 2:00 PM are listed first as the usual alternatives.
  5. For in-person programs, enter the location as City, ST (for example Austin, TX).
  6. Choose Open or Closed enrollment and, if known, the facilitator.
  7. Check the cohort label, for example 2027.01.07 NCIT B CAN Open, and choose Add cohort.
  1. 1Choose the program. Its template sets the sessions and their pattern.
  2. 2Enter the Welcome session date (Day 1 for in-person programs).
  3. 3Online programs default to 1:00 PM Eastern; 11:00 AM and 2:00 PM are listed first.
  4. 4The cohort label is built automatically from the date, course, location and enrollment.
Patterns come from the program templates. For example, NCIT Basic online runs the Welcome session on a Thursday, then Tuesday through Thursday for the next two weeks. If a cohort starts on an unusual weekday, the scheduler says so. Templates can be edited on the Program templates tab.

Use the calendar

  • Quarter view shows all three months, with sessions colored by program track. Holidays and CLEAR days off are shaded.
  • Week view shows sessions by hour, like Outlook. Overlapping sessions sit side by side. Click a date in the quarter view to open its week.
  • Drag a session to another day, or, in week view, to another time. Sessions moved by hand are marked as changed and keep their date when the cohort moves.
  • Click a session to change its date, time or facilitator, see what it overlaps, or delete the cohort.
  • Filter by facilitator or program at the top. The toolbar stays in view as you scroll.
  1. 1Switch between the quarter view and the week view. The toolbar stays at the top as you scroll.
  2. 2Holidays and CLEAR days off are shaded and named.
  3. 3Sessions that need attention have a red outline. Here, one facilitator (Z) has two cohorts at noon. Drag any session to another day.
  1. 1Two overlapping sessions on Thursday. Drag one to a new time, or open it to use a suggested time.

Resolve conflicts

The Conflicts tab lists:

  • Facilitator conflicts: one facilitator on two overlapping sessions.
  • Facilitators on days off: someone scheduled on a day they're unavailable.
  • Overlapping sessions: two cohorts at the same time.
  • Holidays, days off and weekends.

Each one offers a suggested start time that clears the overlap. Overlaps are allowed: choose Keep both as scheduled to set the flag aside.

  1. 1Suggested start times clear the overlap, with a 15-minute gap.
  2. 2Overlaps are allowed. Keep both as scheduled sets the flag aside.

Facilitators and days off

On the Facilitators and days off tab, keep the facilitator list, each facilitator's days off and CLEAR closure days up to date. A facilitator who is off can't be chosen for a cohort or session on those dates. US federal holidays always show, and Canadian holidays can be switched on.

To have someone cover a single online session, open the session and choose them under Facilitator for this session. The session is marked as covered.

2. Program agendas and publishing

Generate program agendas for Novi

  1. In the Program Scheduler, choose Generate program agendas.
  2. Choose Agenda on a cohort to open its agenda, or All in one document for the ticked cohorts.
  3. In the print window, choose Save as PDF. The file name is already set to the cohort label.

Agendas use the same layouts as always. The staff dashboard's Programs tab also has a Generate program agendas button that opens this window.

  1. 1Agenda opens one cohort's agenda, ready to save as a PDF for its Novi event.
  2. 2All in one document prints the ticked cohorts together.

Publish to the Instructor Portal

  1. Choose Publish to Instructor Portal. New and changed cohorts are ticked.
  2. Check the quarter label and choose Publish.
  3. New cohorts arrive in the staff dashboard as drafts that only staff can see.
  1. 1Check the quarter label. Programs are filtered by it in the staff dashboard.
  2. 2Each cohort shows whether it has been published, or changed since.
  3. 3Choose Publish. New cohorts become drafts; published ones are updated.
The scheduler is the source of truth for dates. To change a live program's dates or times, change them in the scheduler and publish again. Each cohort shows Not published, Published or Changed since publishing so you can tell what still needs to go out.

3. Open and announce

  1. On the staff dashboard Overview, review the drafts. Check dates and times on the Programs tab, or open a program's editor.
  2. Choose Open all drafts (it shows how many, for example Open all 10 drafts), or open programs one at a time with their status menu.
  3. Choose Announce programs. Tick the programs to include, add an optional note, use Preview and Send test to me, then send.
  1. 1Notices at the top call out what needs doing, such as announcing open programs.
  2. 2Responses received, and how many sessions have a confirmed instructor.
  3. 3The program's status. Its button moves it to the next step: open, close or reopen.
  1. 1Add an optional note that appears at the top of the email.
  2. 2Preview shows the email exactly as instructors will see it. Send test to me sends it to you first.

Each instructor's announcement lists only the ticked programs that match their teaching approvals and formats. Instructors approved for none of them aren't emailed.

What decides who sees a program

  • Teaching approvals (Instructors tab): the programs each instructor is approved to teach. Anyone approved for a workshop also sees the Workshop Series.
  • Teaches: the format each instructor chose (Online only, In person only, or Both).
  • Instructors with no approvals or format set see everything, so nobody is left out before their record is complete.

4. Collect responses

The response date is set automatically to 30 days before the cohort's first live session. Instructors can change their answers until then; afterwards answers lock.

  • Overview shows how many instructors have responded to each program and how many sessions are confirmed.
  • Responses shows every answer in a grid laid out like the old availability spreadsheet. Click any cell to enter an answer for an instructor who sent it another way; it's recorded as a staff entry.
  • By instructor (on the Responses tab) shows everything one instructor has told you across programs.
  1. 1Each column is an instructor. Click a name to see their answers across all programs.
  2. 2Click any cell to enter an answer for an instructor who sent it another way. Faded answers come from the instructor's whole-program answer.

Reminders

Reminders go out automatically 7 and 2 days before each response date, only to approved instructors who haven't responded to that program. Use Send reminders on the Overview to send one at any other time.

Need a different response date? Open the program's editor, clear Automatic next to Respond by, and choose a date.

5. Select instructors

  1. Open the Selection tab and choose the program.
  2. For each session or module, choose from the instructors marked available. The number after each name is how many slots they already hold this cycle. The ↗ link opens their teaching history and evaluations.
  3. Set each pick's role (Lead, Co-instructor or Backup). New picks start as Tentative, which only staff can see.
  4. When you've decided, set the picks to Confirmed and choose Save selections.
  5. Choose Email selection notices. Each confirmed instructor gets a short email with a View your assignments button.
  1. 1The program header shows the cohort, dates, number of sessions and response date.
  2. 2Each session lists its date, time and Zoom link. Green picks are confirmed; gold picks are tentative and only staff can see them.
  3. 3Set the role and status. Change Tentative to Confirmed, then choose Save selections.
  4. 4Instructors who marked the session available appear as chips, with how many slots they already hold this cycle.
  5. 5Once every session has a confirmed instructor, Download cohort kit is highlighted.

Add staffing notes at the bottom of the tab; they're kept with the date and author. To pick someone who didn't mark the session available, use + Someone else.

Welcome sessions. In ACRG, ELPR and SRA: Staffing a Board, every instructor confirmed for the cohort attends the Welcome session, so it isn't selected separately. In all other programs the Welcome session isn't listed for instructors.

Instructors who can no longer teach after being confirmed call the program facilitator; staff then update the selection here.

6. Prepare the cohort

Zoom links

Zoom meetings are still created in TalentLMS. Every morning the portal reads the meetings on the clearlearning@clearhq.org Zoom account and matches each to its session by date, start time and length. Instructors see the link on their My assignments page.

  • Find Zoom links now (Overview) or Find Zoom links (Selection) runs the check straight away.
  • Choose the right meeting: when two meetings start at the same time, the Overview asks you to pick the right one or paste the link.
  • Missing links: programs starting within a week that still lack links are listed on the Overview, with a box to paste each link.
  • A link typed into a program's editor is marked as entered by hand and is never replaced by the check.
  1. 1The daily check runs every morning. Find Zoom links now runs it straight away.
  2. 2When two meetings start at the same time, choose the one that belongs to the session.
  3. 3Or paste the link yourself. Links entered by hand are never replaced.

Cohort kit

Once every session has a confirmed instructor, the Overview shows Kit ready and the Selection tab's Download cohort kit button is highlighted. The kit is one zip named after the cohort, containing:

  • Survey links as a printable page and a spreadsheet, one per confirmed instructor for each instructor-led session, plus links for the self-paced modules.
  • QR codes for in-person modules.
  • The welcome email, ready to paste into TalentLMS (subject line first), plus a preview.
  • A READ ME file if anything needs attention, such as a missing Zoom link.
  1. 1The READ ME file lists anything to check before using the kit, such as a missing Zoom link or venue details not entered yet.
In-person welcome emails need venue details. Before downloading, open the program's editor and fill in Welcome email details: venue, address, registration and session times, training room, parking and meals, and the contact. City and program dates fill in automatically.

7. Schedule changes

  1. Make the change in the Program Scheduler and publish the cohort again.
  2. The live program updates; instructors' answers stay attached.
  3. Confirmed instructors whose schedule changed are flagged on the Overview and Selection tab.
  4. Choose Send schedule change notices. Each instructor gets their current schedule with the changes marked.
  1. 1Each affected instructor is listed with exactly what changed.

A replaced Zoom link is treated as a change too. A first Zoom link appearing is not, since instructors check My assignments for it.

8. Evaluation reports

The evaluation report tool (/evaluation-reports) works as it always has: filters, data corrections, comment selection, PDF and XLSX exports, and the link to update the Master Instructor File.

  1. Generate the instructor report and deselect any comments that shouldn't be shared.
  2. Choose Publish to Instructor Portal.
  3. Each instructor's report is posted to their Evaluation reports page with its own private link, and they receive an email.
  1. 1Publish to Instructor Portal posts each instructor's report and emails them a link. Only comments left selected are included.
  2. 2See when each instructor opened their report. Withdraw removes it from their portal.

Names that don't match an instructor on the roster are listed instead of published; correct the name and publish again. Reports published to the Instructor Portal, at the bottom of the tool, shows each report, whether it has been viewed, and a Withdraw button.

9. Expenses and hours

Instructors submit their teaching hours and expenses from My assignments in the Instructor Portal. Reports go to CLEAR’s expense system, where they’re reviewed and approved as before.

What instructors see

  • Each confirmed program has an expense button. It opens on the day of the instructor’s last session in that program; before then it shows the date it opens.
  • The form is filled in for them: name, email and pay rate from the expense system’s instructor list (locked); phone and address from their portal profile; the program, delivery method and, for in-person programs, the destination and travel dates; and one teaching day per date they taught, with the scheduled hours and modules.
  • Per diem, travel expenses and receipts work as on the expense form instructors used before.
  • After submitting, the report shows on My assignments with its status: Submitted, then Pending, Approved or Denied.

For coordinators

  • Review and approve in the CLEAR expense admin, as before. Portal reports are marked Submitted via: Instructor Portal and include the cohort label.
  • Rates and totals are set by the system. The pay rate comes from the instructor’s tier in the expense admin, and totals are recalculated before the report is sent, so they can’t be changed on the form.
  • Two-day modules appear on both days without hours; instructors enter the hours they taught each day.
  • To see an instructor’s form, open their Preview page from the Instructors tab and choose the expense button. Submitting is turned off in a preview.
Keep instructor emails matched. The portal finds each instructor’s pay rate by matching their sign-in email to their entry in the expense admin’s instructor list. If an instructor’s details don’t fill in, check that their email there matches the one on the Instructors tab.

Instructor and staff records

Instructors tab

  • Contact details: each instructor's name (Last, First), every email they may sign in with, active status, notes, and the format they teach (Teaches). Instructors set their own format in the portal; staff can change it here.
  • Teaching approvals: tick the programs each instructor is approved to teach.
  • Paste names and emails adds or updates several instructors at once, for example from the master instructor file. Each instructor has a Preview page link that shows their portal exactly as they see it.
  1. 1An instructor with nothing ticked sees every program until their approvals are set.

Instructor profiles

Instructors keep their own profile in the portal: full name with suffix and preferred name, job title, employer, phone, contact email, mailing address, headshot and professional bio.

  • Choose Profile next to an instructor to see it, download their headshot, or copy their bio with its formatting.
  • A Check flag means the profile’s name differs from the roster name, or their contact email isn’t one of their sign-in addresses. Evaluation reports and expense reports match on the roster name and sign-in emails, so update those here if the change should apply everywhere. Editing a profile never changes the roster.
  • Export profiles downloads every profile as a spreadsheet.
  1. 1A notice appears when the profile’s name or email differs from the roster, with what to update.
  2. 2Their headshot, with a full-size download.
  3. 3Copy bio keeps the formatting, ready to paste into Word or Novi.
Adding or removing an instructor also needs an access change. After saving, use the email link in the notice on the Instructors tab to tell the administrator, so the person can be added to or removed from the Cloudflare Zero Trust access group. The Staff tab has the same notice for staff changes.

Other tabs

  • Programs: every program grouped by type. Edit details, status, response date, track, logo, sessions and Zoom links, or add a one-off program.
  • Staff: who can use the dashboard. Only admins can change it.
  • Activity: a log of changes, sends and imports.

Troubleshooting

If…Then…
An instructor can't sign in or doesn't get a codeCheck their email is listed on the Instructors tab and that the administrator has added it to the Zero Trust access group. Codes can land in junk mail.
An instructor can't see a programCheck their teaching approvals and format, and that the program is Open. Use their Preview page link to see what they see.
A program shows "Answers locked"The response date has passed. Change it in the program's editor, or enter answers for the instructor on the Responses tab.
A session has no Zoom linkCheck the meeting exists in TalentLMS for that date and time, then choose Find Zoom links. If it still doesn't match, paste the link on the Overview.
The scheduler says someone else changed the scheduleChoose Load their version to see the latest, or Keep my version to save yours over theirs.
The agenda window doesn't openAllow pop-ups for the portal in your browser, then try again.
An instructor can't find the expense button, or it says it isn't openReports open on the day of their last confirmed session. Check they're Confirmed on the Selection tab for that program.
An instructor's name, pay rate or details don't fill in on the expense formTheir email in the expense admin's instructor list doesn't match the email they sign in with. Update it in the expense admin.
A report name didn't match the rosterFix the name with a data correction in the report tool, or on the Instructors tab, then publish again.
