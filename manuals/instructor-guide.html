Instructor Portal Guide

The Instructor Portal is where you see upcoming CLEAR Learning programs, tell us which sessions you can teach, and find everything for the sessions you're confirmed to teach, including Zoom links and your evaluation reports.

How it works

Each quarter, CLEAR Learning schedules the next quarter's programs and posts them to the portal. The process from there:

New programs

You receive an email when programs that match your instruction preferences are open.

You respond

Mark the sessions you can and can't teach by each program's response date.

Selection

After the response date, CLEAR Learning confirms instructors and emails a selection notice.

Get ready

Your sessions, times and Zoom links are on My assignments, with calendar downloads. Program materials are on Program files.

Your report

After the program, submit your expenses and hours from My assignments. Your evaluation report appears on Evaluation reports.

Signing in

There's no password. You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email.

  1. Open the Instructor Portal using the button at the top of this page or the link in any CLEAR Learning email. Bookmark it for next time.
  2. Enter the email address CLEAR Learning has on file for you and choose Send me a code.
  3. Check your email for the code (it may take a minute, and may land in junk mail) and enter it.
  1. 1Enter the email address CLEAR Learning has on file for you.
  2. 2Enter the one-time code from your email.
Using a different email address? The portal only recognizes addresses on file. If your code never arrives, or you see a message that your email isn't on the list, contact clearlearning@clearhq.org.

Your formats

At the top of the Programs page, tell us which formats you are available to teach: Online only, In person only or Both.

You'll see, and receive emails about, programs in the formats you choose. You can change this at any time. Until you choose, you'll see every program.

  1. 1Until you choose, this bar is highlighted. Once you choose, it turns white; you can change it at any time.

Finding programs

The Programs page shows a card for each program that matches your instruction preferences, with its logo, cohort, dates, session times and response date. A colored band across the top of each card shows the program family.

Each card shows where your answers stand:

  • Needs your response You haven't answered yet.
  • 3 of 7 answered You've answered some sessions.
  • ✓ Responded You've answered every session. The session list turns green.
  1. 1The program's logo, family color, cohort label, dates and format.
  2. 2Session dates and times. The list turns green once you've answered every session.
  3. 3The response date and days remaining. It turns red in the last week.
  4. 4Your status: Needs your response, 3 of 7 answered, or Responded.

Use Show to see everything, only programs that need your response, or programs you've already answered. The response date turns red when it's a week or less away.

Answering

  1. Open a program's card.
  2. Use the Whole program row to answer every session at once, for example AvailableNot availableN/A
  3. Change any single session that's different. Your answer for that session overrides the whole-program answer.
  4. Add anything we should know in Comments for staff, for example "first half only" or a session you'd especially like to lead.
  5. Choose Save answers. A bar at the bottom of the screen reminds you if you have unsaved changes.
  1. 1Answer for the whole program in one click.
  2. 2Change any session that's different. Its own answer overrides the whole-program answer.
  3. 3Add anything staff should know.
  4. 4Save. You can come back and change your answers until the response date.

Not available helps us as much as Available: it tells us we've heard from you. Use N/A for a session that doesn't apply to you.

Changing your answers

You can change your answers at any time before the response date (11:59 PM Eastern). After that, answers are locked while instructors are selected.

In-person programs

In-person programs are staffed by module. Each module is listed with its day and time, in the venue's local time. Some modules run across two days.

Welcome sessions

In ACRG, ELPR and SRA: Staffing a Board, every instructor confirmed for the cohort attends the Welcome session. It's marked All instructors assigned to this cohort attend and doesn't need an answer. It's added to your assignments automatically if you're confirmed.

Time zones

All times in the portal are shown in Eastern Time, the time zone CLEAR schedules in. You have the option to change the time zone to one of your choosing:

  1. On the time zone bar near the top of the page, choose Show times in my time zone. To use a different time zone, pick it from the list that appears.
  2. Times are converted, with the original Eastern Time shown in brackets. If a session falls on a different day for you, that's marked too.
  3. Choose Back to Eastern Time at any point.
  1. 1Choose Show times in my time zone, or pick any time zone from the list. Back to Eastern Time returns to the default.
  2. 2Each time shows in your time zone, with Eastern Time in brackets.

Changing the time zone only changes what you see; your answers are the same either way. In-person programs always show the venue's local time.

My assignments

Once you're confirmed to teach, your sessions appear on My assignments with the program, cohort, date, time and your role. This is the place to check for everything about the sessions you're teaching.

  • Zoom links appear on each online session before the program's first session. Until then, the session shows The Zoom link appears here before the first session.
  • Add to calendar (.ics) downloads a calendar file with your sessions. If you download it before the Zoom links appear, download it again afterwards to include them.
  1. 1Download a calendar file with your sessions. Download it again after Zoom links appear to include them.
  2. 2Each session shows its date, time, your role and, once available, its Zoom link.
Can no longer teach a session you're confirmed for? Please call the program facilitator as soon as possible. Changes after confirmation are handled by phone so nothing is missed.

Expenses and hours

After you teach, submit your teaching hours, and for an in-person program your meal per diem and travel expenses, from My assignments. There's one report per program, and it goes straight to CLEAR Learning for approval.

  1. 1Once you've submitted a report, the button changes to Submit another report, for anything you need to add.
  2. 2Each report you've submitted shows its total and its status: Submitted, Pending, Approved or Denied, with the date it was decided.
  3. 3Before your last session, the button shows the date it opens.

Filling in the form

  1. On My assignments, choose Submit expenses and hours beside the program. The form opens on the day of your last session; before then you can look at it, but you can't submit it yet.
  2. Your information. Your name and email come from CLEAR's records and are locked. Check your phone number and mailing address: you can correct them for this report, and to update your records permanently, email clearlearning@clearhq.org.
  3. Teaching hours. The program and delivery method are filled in and locked. Under Instruction Time, check each day's hours and modules, which come from your schedule. Change any hours that were different and enter hours for any day left blank, such as a module that ran across two days. Your hourly rate is shown, and your teaching pay is worked out from it.
  4. For an in-person program, complete the per diem, expense and receipt sections described below.
  5. Check the Expense Summary, then choose Submit Report. The button turns on once every required field (marked *) is complete. You'll see a report ID, and the report's status appears on My assignments.
  1. 1Your name, email, the program and its delivery method are filled in for you and can't be changed. Check your phone number and address.
  2. 2One row for each day, with the hours and modules from your schedule. Correct the hours if they were different, and fill in any day left blank.
  3. 3If a meal was provided, tick the box and choose which meal or meals. That day's per diem is reduced automatically.
  4. 4Add a line for each travel expense, then upload your receipts as one PDF.
  5. 5The summary adds up your teaching pay, per diem and expenses as you go.

In-person programs: per diem, expenses and receipts

  • Trip details: your destination is filled in, and your departure and return dates are set to the day before your first session and the day after your last. Change the dates if you travelled on different days; the per diem and teaching days update to match.
  • Meal per diem is applied automatically for each day of your trip: the full amount on full days and a reduced amount on your departure and return days. No meal receipts are needed. If CLEAR or the program host provided a meal, tick Meal Provided? for that day and choose which meal or meals; the amount is reduced to match.
  • Expense lines: add one line for each expense, such as hotel, airfare, rental car, parking or rideshare. For hotels, airfare and rental cars, put the vendor's name in the description. A new blank line appears as you fill in each one.
  • Mileage: choose Mileage, describe the trip (for example, home to airport) and enter the miles. The amount is calculated at CLEAR's mileage rate.
  • Other currencies: if you paid in another currency, choose it and enter the amount in the Original Currency column; it's converted to US dollars at the current exchange rate.
  • Receipts: upload receipts for your expense lines (not meals) as a single PDF, up to 20 MB. If you have several receipts, combine or scan them into one PDF first.
Already submitted? The form tells you if you've submitted a report for this program. Submit another only to report something the first one didn't include.
Details not filled in? If the form says it couldn't find your details in CLEAR's expense records, enter your phone and address and submit as normal; CLEAR Learning will confirm your rate. If your name or rate looks wrong, email clearlearning@clearhq.org.

Evaluation reports

After a program, CLEAR Learning publishes your evaluation report to the Evaluation reports page and emails you a link. Each report summarizes participant ratings by module and the comments shared with you.

  • Choose View report to open it in a new tab. New reports are marked New until you open them.
  • To keep a copy, use your browser's print option and choose Save as PDF.
  • Your reports are private: only you and CLEAR Learning staff can open them.
  1. 1New reports are marked New until you open them.
  2. 2View report opens it in a new tab. To keep a copy, print it and choose Save as PDF.

Program files

The Program files page has the materials for every program you're approved to teach: slide decks, guides and other files. They come straight from CLEAR Learning's library, so a download here is always the current version, and you no longer need to wait for decks by email.

  • Each program is its own section. Some are divided under headings, such as US Virtual, CAN Virtual or In-Person, so you can find the version you're teaching.
  • Choose Download to save a file to your computer, usually in your Downloads folder. Open it in PowerPoint, a PDF reader or whichever program suits the file.
  • Updated means a file has changed since you last downloaded it. Download it again to get the new version. The number on the Program files tab shows how many of your files have been updated.
  • Files you already have show the date you downloaded them.
  1. 1The number on the tab shows how many of your files have been updated since you downloaded them.
  2. 2Larger programs are divided under headings, such as the US and Canadian versions. Choose a program's name to show or hide its files.
  3. 3Updated means the file changed after you downloaded it. Download it again for the current version.
Download your materials again shortly before you teach. Files can be updated between cohorts, so check for Updated badges in the days before your session to be sure you're presenting the latest version.

You'll see the materials for every program you're approved to teach, not only the ones you're confirmed for, so you can prepare or refresh at any time.

Common questions

I don't see a program I expected.

The portal shows programs that match your instruction preferences and the formats you chose. Check your format at the top of the Programs page, and set Show to Everything. If it's still missing, contact us.

The response date has passed and I need to change an answer.

Email clearlearning@clearhq.org with the change.

I'm confirmed but there's no Zoom link yet.

Links appear before the program's first session. If a link is still missing a few days before your session, contact us.

I don't see the materials for a program I teach.

Program files shows the programs you're approved to teach. If one is missing, or a file you need isn't there, email clearlearning@clearhq.org.

How do I know I have the latest version of a deck?

If a file shows Updated, it has changed since you downloaded it, so download it again. A file showing only the date you downloaded it hasn't changed since.

I can't find the Submit expenses and hours button.

It's on My assignments, beside each program you're confirmed for, and opens on the day of your last session in that program. Before then it shows the date it opens.

Do I need receipts?

Not for meals: the per diem needs no receipts. For travel expenses on an in-person program, upload your receipts as one PDF with your expense report.

Can I use the portal on my phone?

Yes. The portal works on phones, tablets and computers. You can download program files on a phone too, though slide decks are easiest to open and present from a computer.

Who do I contact?

For questions about the portal, email clearlearning@clearhq.org or call 859-269-1289. If you need to withdraw from a confirmed session, please call the program facilitator.
