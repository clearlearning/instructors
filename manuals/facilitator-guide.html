Facilitator Portal Guide

The Facilitator Portal is where you see CLEAR Learning sessions that are available to facilitate, tell us which ones you can cover, and find everything for the sessions you’re confirmed to facilitate, including Zoom links, program materials and your hours reports.

How it works

CLEAR Learning posts the online sessions that are open to contract facilitators. The process from there:

Sessions posted

You receive an email when sessions are available in the programs you’re approved to facilitate.

You respond

Mark the sessions you can and can’t facilitate by each program’s response date.

Selection

After the response date, CLEAR Learning confirms facilitators and emails a selection notice.

Get ready

Your sessions, Zoom links and instructors are on My assignments; materials are on Program files.

Your hours

After your last session in a program, submit your hours from My assignments.

Signing in

There’s no password. You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email.

  1. Open the Facilitator Portal using the button at the top of this page or the link in any CLEAR Learning email. The address ends in /facilitator; bookmark it for next time.
  2. Enter the email address CLEAR Learning has on file for you and choose Send me a code.
  3. Check your email for the code (it may take a minute, and may land in junk mail) and enter it.
  1. 1Enter the email address CLEAR Learning has on file for you.
  2. 2Enter the one-time code from your email.
Using a different email address? The portal only recognizes addresses on file. If your code never arrives, or you see a message that your email isn’t on the list, contact clearlearning@clearhq.org.

Available sessions

The Available sessions page shows each online program with sessions open to contract facilitators, for the programs you’re approved to facilitate. Only the sessions you can pick up are listed.

  1. 1The program, cohort label, dates and how many of its sessions are open to you.
  2. 2The sessions you can facilitate, with their dates and times.
  3. 3The response date and days remaining.
  4. 4Your status: Needs your response, partly answered, or Responded.

Use Show to see everything, only programs that need your response, or programs you’ve already answered. The response date turns red when it’s a week or less away.

Answering

  1. Open a program.
  2. Use the Whole program row to answer every session at once.
  3. Change any single session that’s different. Your answer for that session overrides the whole-program answer.
  4. Add anything we should know in Comments for staff.
  5. Choose Save answers.
  1. 1Answer for the whole program in one click.
  2. 2Change any session that's different. Its own answer overrides the whole-program answer.
  3. 3Add anything staff should know.
  4. 4Save. You can come back and change your answers until the response date.

Not available helps us as much as Available: it tells us we’ve heard from you. You can change your answers at any time before the response date (11:59 PM Eastern). After that, answers are locked while facilitators are selected.

Time zones

All times are shown in Eastern Time, the time zone CLEAR schedules in. You have the option to change the time zone to one of your choosing:

  1. On the time zone bar near the top of the page, choose Show times in my time zone. To use a different time zone, pick it from the list that appears.
  2. Times are converted, with the original Eastern Time shown in brackets.
  3. Choose Back to Eastern Time at any point.
  1. 1Choose Show times in my time zone, or pick any time zone from the list. Back to Eastern Time returns to the default.
  2. 2Each time shows in your time zone, with Eastern Time in brackets.

My assignments

Once you’re confirmed to facilitate, your sessions appear on My assignments with the program, cohort, date and time.

  1. 1Download a calendar file with your sessions.
  2. 2Each session shows its date, time and, once available, its Zoom link.
  3. 3The instructor confirmed for the session.
  4. 4Submit your hours after your last session in the program.
  • Instructors: each session shows the instructor confirmed to teach it, or To be confirmed.
  • Zoom links appear on each session before the program’s first session.
  • Add to calendar (.ics) downloads a calendar file with your sessions. If you download it before the Zoom links appear, download it again afterwards to include them.
Can no longer facilitate a session you’re confirmed for? Please contact the program facilitator at CLEAR Learning as soon as possible. Changes after confirmation are handled by phone so nothing is missed.

Program files

The Program files page has the materials for the programs you’re approved to facilitate, straight from CLEAR Learning’s library, so a download here is always the current version.

  1. 1The number on the tab shows how many of your files have been updated since you downloaded them.
  2. 2Larger programs are divided under headings, such as the US and Canadian versions. Choose a program's name to show or hide its files.
  3. 3Updated means the file changed after you downloaded it. Download it again for the current version.
  • Each program is its own section, sometimes divided under headings such as US Virtual or CAN Virtual.
  • Choose Download to save a file. Files marked Updated have changed since you last downloaded them.

Submitting your hours

After you facilitate, submit your hours from My assignments. Each program has its own report, and most of it is filled in for you. The report covers hours only: there are no travel expenses or per diem, and every facilitated program is online, so there’s no delivery method to choose.

  1. From the day of your last session in a program, choose Submit hours on that program. Before then, the button shows the date it opens.
  2. Check your details. Your name and email come from CLEAR’s records, and your hourly rate comes from CLEAR’s facilitator pay tiers.
  3. Under Facilitation Hours, the program you facilitated is filled in for you. Check the days you facilitated, the hours for each day and the sessions listed, and correct anything that’s different.
  4. Choose Submit Report.
  1. 1The program, cohort and the dates you facilitated.
  2. 2Your name and email come from CLEAR’s records. You can correct your phone and address for this report.
  3. 3The program is filled in for you. Your hourly rate, from CLEAR’s facilitator pay tiers, is shown above it.
  4. 4One row for each day you facilitated, with the scheduled hours and the sessions. Check them and correct anything that’s different.
  5. 5Submit. The report’s status then appears on My assignments.

Your report goes to CLEAR’s expense team for review. Its status appears on My assignments: Submitted, then Pending, Approved or Denied. To report something you left out, choose Submit another report on the same program.

My profile

Keep your name, contact details and mailing address current on My profile. CLEAR Learning uses them to contact you and on your hours reports.

  1. 1Your full name, with a suffix such as Jr. if you use one.
  2. 2The phone number and email CLEAR Learning uses to contact you.
  3. 3Your mailing address, used on your hours reports.
  4. 4Save. Your phone and address fill in your hours reports automatically.

The email on your profile is where CLEAR Learning contacts you. It doesn’t change the email you sign in with; to change that, contact clearlearning@clearhq.org.

Common questions

I don’t see a program I expected.

The Available sessions page shows online sessions that are open to contract facilitators in the programs you’re approved to facilitate. Some sessions are facilitated by CLEAR staff and aren’t offered. If you expected to see a program, contact us.

The response date has passed and I need to change an answer.

Email clearlearning@clearhq.org with the change.

I’m confirmed but there’s no Zoom link yet.

Links appear before the program’s first session. If a link is still missing a few days before your session, contact us.

I don’t see the materials for a program.

Program files shows the folders for the programs you’re approved to facilitate. If a program or file is missing, email clearlearning@clearhq.org.

Can I use the portal on my phone?

Yes. The portal works on phones, tablets and computers.

Who do I contact?

For questions about the portal, email clearlearning@clearhq.org or call 859-269-1289. If you need to withdraw from a confirmed session, please contact CLEAR Learning’s program facilitator.
